# Provo CySA+ changelog

## v0.1.0
- First release of Provo CySA+ for CompTIA CySA+ CS0-004, built from the official CS0-004 Exam Objectives (v2.0) and weighted 34 / 26 / 24 / 16 across the four domains.
- 14 modules: Acronyms (97), eleven topic modules covering every objective from 1.1 to 4.2 (243 terms), Question Qualifiers (25 analyst scenarios), and Mini-Exams drawn from 95 scenario questions.
- Covers what is new in CS0-004, including AI in security operations (1.6), EPSS, SBOM and SCA, STRIDE, the Pyramid of Pain, LOLBins, business email compromise, OTX, MISP, OpenCTI, YARA, Zeek, Suricata, Trivy, Checkov, Atomic Red Team, and Caldera.
- Every question explains why the correct answer is right and why each other option is wrong. Mini-exams give a full review when you finish.
- A one-page Cram Sheet with the incident response order, CVSS versus EPSS versus KEV, the Pyramid of Pain, the attack frameworks, a tool-to-job map, key Windows event IDs, evidence handling, and reporting metrics.
