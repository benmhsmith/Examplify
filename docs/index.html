How it works

  1. Open your examRuns in any browser, phone included. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Work the stages in orderVocabulary first, then core skills, troubleshooting, exam technique, and practice exams.
  3. Earn your ranksReach Expert in every module to earn the Mastery Badge. Reach Pro to perfect a module.

About the creator

BS

Ben SmithIT instructor and creator of Provo

I teach IT certification courses to adults who are brand new to IT, taking them from their first A+ lesson through Network+, Security+ and CySA+ in an intensive evening program.

I built Provo because my students needed somewhere to practice between classes that tells them honestly when they're ready to book the exam, not just how many questions they've answered. Every question, lab and explanation is written against the official CompTIA exam objectives and shaped by what I see students struggle with in class.

Your progress stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to contact us or report an issue. Found a wrong answer or have an idea? Tell us on GitHub.
